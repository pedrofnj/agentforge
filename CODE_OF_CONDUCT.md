# Code of Conduct

AgentForge follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

- Be respectful and welcoming, in English or Portuguese.
- Give and accept constructive, specific feedback; assume good intent.
- Focus on the work: code, evidence and user impact.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualized content, doxxing or publishing others'
  private information.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to anyone
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the contact options on
[github.com/pedrofnj](https://github.com/pedrofnj). Maintainers may edit or remove contributions
and temporarily or permanently ban participants who violate this code. Reports are handled
confidentially.
